Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Milkywayy collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our website, booking services, customer dashboard, and connected ChatGPT GPT actions.

Effective date: June 30, 2026
Website: https://milkywayy.com
Contact: hello@milkywayy.ae

Information we collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and company details
  • Booking details, property details, invoices, and delivery metadata
  • Messages you send through contact forms, email, or WhatsApp
  • Account and login information needed to authenticate and secure your access

How we use information

  • Provide and operate the Milkywayy booking and delivery platform
  • Authenticate users and protect customer accounts
  • Process bookings, invoices, and service communications
  • Support customers and respond to questions or issues
  • Monitor, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

ChatGPT GPT actions and OAuth access

If you choose to connect Milkywayy to a ChatGPT GPT using OAuth, you are explicitly authorizing that GPT to access the Milkywayy data scopes shown on the consent screen.

  • The current GPT integration is intended to access read-only customer data, such as account details, bookings, invoices, and delivery-file metadata.
  • Milkywayy uses OAuth tokens to authorize that access and logs security events needed to protect the integration.
  • You can revoke a connected GPT connection from the Milkywayy customer dashboard when that control is available to your account.
  • Connecting a GPT is optional. If you do not connect it, the GPT cannot access your Milkywayy account data through OAuth.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as needed with service providers, infrastructure partners, payment providers, or professional advisers who help us operate the service, or when required by law or to protect rights and security.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or security obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect data, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

  • You may contact us to ask about the personal information we hold.
  • You may request corrections or deletion, subject to contractual, legal, or security requirements.
  • You may stop using a connected GPT integration by revoking its access.

Contact us

For privacy questions or support related to this policy, contact hello@milkywayy.ae.
